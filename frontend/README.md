# XMAX AI Inc PRD Web

A visually rich single-page website for XMAX AI Inc., highlighting global AI inference infrastructure, AWS foundation, governed products, deployment planning, security, and enterprise industry programs.

## Application-area content

Document item 10 is implemented by removing the public claim of "9 Business Units", not by inventing evidence of nine operating businesses. `src/content/business.ts` supplies the illustrative application descriptions used by the business directory, all nine detail routes, home-page slides, About, navigation, and footer. Each area identifies users/data, proposed AI outputs, and human review boundaries. Existing CMS media, aliases, and the About legal-entity table are retained.

The frontend applies this copy after loading CMS data, so legacy CMS wording does not replace the remediation. CMS records and backend seed/repair scripts have not been rewritten. The unrelated `30+`, `100+`, and `99.9%` statistics and AWS content remain unchanged. Any CMS migration should preserve this editorial layer until business owners approve evidence-backed replacement copy. See `../BUSINESS_CONTENT_REMEDIATION.md` for the decision and verification checklist.
